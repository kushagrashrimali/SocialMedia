# WYSTAK pre-launch teaser (single image, 1080×1350)

A name reveal on a real city billboard. No product information. Rebuild with `python3 wystak/instagram/single-posts/prelaunch/build.py`.

## How it is made

A real photograph of a blank billboard is the base (`assets/billboard-photo-unsplash-t0lLGHhnh8w.jpg`, Photo by personalgraphic.com on Unsplash, free to use under the Unsplash licence). The Wystak screen (black LED screen, "Something new is stacking up…", three lit cards in navy, violet and teal, and a white "COMING SOON" band with the supplied wordmark) is rendered in code, placed on the billboard face with the photo's perspective, and given the glow, light spill and sky reflection a real LED screen has. The supplied logo wordmark is used as is.

## Caption

Say hello to Wystak. Coming soon.

Follow along and be the first to see it. www.wystak.com
