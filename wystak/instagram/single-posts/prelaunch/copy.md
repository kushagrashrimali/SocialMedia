# WYSTAK pre-launch teaser (single image, 1080×1350)

A torn-paper reveal. No product information. Rebuild with `python3 wystak/instagram/single-posts/prelaunch/build.py`.

## The idea

A flat 2D poster that reads as 3D. A pale lilac paper sheet is torn open and rolled back, and the supplied Wystak logo peeks through the opening, with its right side tucked behind the roll. "SOMETHING NEW IS STACKING UP…" above, "STAY TUNED!" and www.wystak.com below.

## How the 3D illusion is built (all drawn in code)

- a ragged tear with a lighter, fibrous rim on both torn edges, roughened with an SVG displacement filter;
- soft cast shadows under both edges and from the roll onto the white surface beneath;
- a cylindrical roll of paper with a layered end, lit from the top left;
- a paper-grain overlay multiplied over the whole sheet, and a light falloff across it.

The logo is the supplied file, unaltered. Type: Krona One and Poppins.
