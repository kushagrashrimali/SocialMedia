## Post text

Apple Pay is not live in India. Wallet passes still work.

So many people assume Apple Wallet does not work here either. It does.

Loyalty passes work in Apple Wallet on iPhone and in Google Wallet on Android, with no app to install.

That matters because both kinds of phone stand in the same queue at the same counter. A loyalty programme for a café, a grocery store or a salon has to work for everyone in it.

That is how we are building Wystak:

• One programme, both wallets
• Customers tap or scan at the counter to join
• Points update on the lock screen within seconds of every visit
• The owner sees who keeps coming back, and who stopped

If you build for Indian merchants (POS, billing, payments), or you run a counter yourself, we would like to hear from you. Link in the comments.
