## Post text

Every phone already has a wallet. Your café is not in it.

Boarding passes and event tickets live there. A neighbourhood loyalty programme does not.

Big chains already run loyalty programmes through specialist providers. The café, grocery store or salon down the road is rarely served.

We are building Wystak to close that gap. Wystak puts a merchant's loyalty pass in the customer's Apple Wallet or Google Wallet:

• No app to install
• Customers tap or scan at the counter to join
• Points update on the lock screen within seconds of every visit
• The owner sees who keeps coming back, and who stopped

We are starting with the merchants every neighbourhood runs on: cafés, grocery stores and salons.

If you run one, or build tools for them (POS, billing, payments), we would like to talk. Link in the comments.
