## Post text

Would you know if your best regular stopped coming?

An owner can read the best-seller off the till. The regular who quietly stopped coming does not show up anywhere.

By the time it is obvious, that customer has usually found somewhere else.

We are building Wystak so owners can see it earlier. Wystak puts the loyalty pass in the customer's Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, with no app to install:

• Customers tap or scan at the counter to join
• Points land on the lock screen within seconds of every visit
• The owner sees who keeps coming back, and who stopped

For a café, a grocery store or a salon, that is the difference between guessing and knowing.

How do you keep track of your regulars today? We would like to hear it.
