# WYSTAK prospects, week of 9 to 15 November 2026

Focus: **Indiranagar** this week (the beachhead is one Bengaluru neighbourhood), with Koramangala and HSR as the next stops.
Ranked by fit: independent or small-group, owner-run, frequent visits, new or growing. These are researched leads, not verified contacts.
Before you reach out, check each café's opening date, address and owner on their Instagram or Google listing.

## How to approach (suggested)

- **Mon to Tue:** DM Tier 1 on Instagram from the WYSTAK account, using the opening line, and ask for 15 minutes at the café.
- **Wed to Fri:** visit Indiranagar between 3 and 5 pm, when it's quiet. Show the pass live on a phone: scan the QR, add it to the wallet, scan it again, and the points land on the lock screen.
- **Bring:** the counter-stand mock-up and a sample pass in each café's colours (build them from their logo the night before).
- **Log each touch** (date, channel, response) so a follow-up goes out 3 days later.
- Don't quote prices or numbers. Offer a free walkthrough and a no-card trial.
