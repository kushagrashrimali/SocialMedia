# WYSTAK email rules

- Write to the owner of one independent café, bakery or restaurant in India. Indian English, plain words, short sentences.
- Email 1: under 80 words. One specific observation about their café (from their own site or listing), one line on what WYSTAK does, one easy question. No link in email 1.
- Never invent numbers, customers, results or prices. No statistics at all.
- Never say or imply that customers won't share a phone number, or anything about collecting phone numbers.
- Never build the email on lost or forgotten paper loyalty cards; India has no stamp-card habit.
- Show Apple Wallet and Google Wallet with equal weight. Apple Pay and Apple Wallet work in India; never imply otherwise.
- No gendered pronouns (he, she, his, her, him). Use the role ("your regulars", "the owner") or "they".
- Allowed claims only: no app, scan (or tap NFC) at the counter to add the pass, points on the lock screen within seconds of a scan, the owner sees who comes back and who stopped.
- The ask is a 15-minute call or a quick visit to show the pass with their own branding. Offer to drop by.
- Sign off as Kushagra, WYSTAK, hello@wystak.com.
