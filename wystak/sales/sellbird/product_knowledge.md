# WYSTAK product knowledge

WYSTAK (pronounced "WHYS-TAK"). Tagline: ALL YOUR PASSES. ONE STACK. Site: https://www.wystak.com. Contact: hello@wystak.com.

## What it is
Digital loyalty cards for cafés and restaurants inside Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. No app for the customer.

## How it works
1. The customer scans the café's QR (counter, receipt, table tent) or taps the NFC stand.
2. A short form, then "Add to Wallet": the café's branded card is in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
3. Staff scan the pass at each visit with any phone, tablet or laptop camera (web scanner). Manual code entry if a scan fails.
4. Points or stamps update within seconds and show on the lock screen.

## Why a café owner cares
After a UPI payment nothing of the café goes home with the customer. The pass does: it sits next to their cards, and every visit puts the café back on the lock screen. Branded apps don't get downloaded; WhatsApp blasts get muted; notebooks don't get updated.

## Dashboard
Total members and new sign-ups, visits and redemptions over time, customers one stamp away from a reward, lapsed customers worth reaching out to.

## Setup
Minutes. No POS integration, no extra hardware, no training. Custom logo, colours and reward copy. No credit card required to try.

## Target
Independent cafés and bakeries in Bengaluru first (one neighbourhood at a time), then QSR and casual dining, then gyms and salons.

## Not to claim
No statistics, prices, customer names or traction in outreach. Pricing is shared on a call.
