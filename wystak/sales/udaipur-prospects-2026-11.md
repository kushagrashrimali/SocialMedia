# Who to approach in Udaipur: week of 9 to 14 November 2026

You're local, so every lead is meant for a walk-in, an Instagram DM or a WhatsApp message. No cold email.
Researched from Google-indexed listings, local blogs and OpenStreetMap. Before each visit, check that the business is open, where it is and who owns it.

## What makes a good WYSTAK customer

1. **Locals who come back often.** The more visits, the more each point and lock-screen update gets seen. Daily or weekly visits are best.
2. **One owner who decides** at the counter. Not a franchise or a head office.
3. **A reason to want regulars**: a new place building a crowd, several outlets, or a crowded street with lots of competition.

So skip the Old City rooftop restaurants around Lal Ghat, Gangaur Ghat and Lake Pichola Road (most of what OpenStreetMap lists). Their guests are tourists who come once.
Also skip national chains (CCD, Talwalkars, Looks Salon, Apollo): head office decides.

## What to bring and say

- A phone with a demo pass in **that business's own name and colours** (make it the night before from their Instagram logo). Scan, add to the wallet, scan again, and the points land on the lock screen. Showing it beats explaining it.
- A printed counter-stand mock-up with a QR (and NFC).
- **One-line pitch:** "When your regulars pay by UPI, nothing of yours goes home with them. This puts your card in their Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, no app, and every visit pops up on their lock screen."
- **The ask:** "Can we set it up free at your counter for a few weeks?"
- Don't quote prices or invented numbers. Note each visit (date, who you met, reply, next step) in `udaipur-prospects-tracker.csv`.

## Find more businesses with Sellbird

```bash
sellbird discover               # free OpenStreetMap search across the 18 types in the config
sellbird export --all           # CSV of everything it found (name, address, phone, site)
sellbird companies              # every business, with a fit score and why
```

OpenStreetMap's Udaipur coverage is thin and leans towards tourist places. For a fuller list, use a paid source at about $0.37 per 1,000 businesses: `sellbird discover --provider dataforseo_listings --max-spend 2.00`. It shows the cost before it runs.
