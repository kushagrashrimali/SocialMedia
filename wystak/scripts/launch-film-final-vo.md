# WYSTAK launch reel: FINAL voiceover (merchant-facing, 35s)

Audience: café, restaurant and shop **owners**, the people who buy Wystak. Earlier customer-side drafts are replaced by this one.

## Concept

**"Your regulars didn't forget you. They forgot your loyalty card."** Every owner has run some kind of loyalty: printed cards, an app, WhatsApp offers. They decided it "doesn't work" when really their customers lost track of it. Meanwhile the regulars still come in every week, and the owner only knows them as a line in the UPI history. Wystak puts the shop's loyalty where customers can't lose it, in their phone's wallet, and gives the owner back what they lost: who keeps coming back, and who quietly stopped. The ending turns the problem into a one-line instruction an owner can repeat: *Be in their wallet. Not their drawer.*

## Final voiceover

```
Your regulars didn't forget you. [BEAT]
They forgot your loyalty card.

It's in a drawer. The app got deleted.
Your WhatsApp offer? Under the good morning forwards.

They still come every week.
To you, they're a line in your UPI history. [PAUSE]

Wystak puts your loyalty in their phone's wallet.
One scan at your counter. No app.
Seconds after they pay, points land on their lock screen.

You see who keeps coming back. [BEAT]
And who quietly stopped. [PAUSE]

Be in their [EMPHASIS] wallet. Not their drawer.
Wystak. All your passes. One stack.
```

## Delivery

- One owner talking to another: calm, knowing, a little dry. Never a sales pitch.
- About 160 wpm. The hook is quick and confident, with a beat before "They forgot your loyalty card."
- "Under the good morning forwards" is deadpan; let it get a smile.
- "To you, they're a line in your UPI history" is slower and quieter. This is the sting, so pause after it.
- The product lines are crisp and matter-of-fact.
- "And who quietly stopped" is softer, almost an aside, then a pause.
- "Be in their **wallet**. Not their drawer." is slow and even. The tagline is settled and falls at the end.
- ElevenLabs Multilingual v2: Stability 50–60 · Similarity 75 · Style 10–20 · Speed 1.0 · WAV export. A warm Indian-English or neutral voice.

## Truth check

- Every claim is supported: no app, one scan to add the pass, Apple and Google Wallet ("their phone's wallet"), points on the lock screen within seconds of a scan, and the owner sees returning and lapsed customers (pitch deck slide 3).
- No statistics, prices, customers or traction.
- The "loyalty card" is the physical one being replaced; in the wallet it's a pass.
- WhatsApp and UPI are named as everyday context only; no real brand logos appear on screen. Fictional merchant: Paper Crane Coffee.
