# WYSTAK launch reel: final script (merchant-facing, about 33s)

Audience: café, restaurant and shop owners in India. There are no loyalty cards in the story, because most Indian shops never had them. The problem is what owners actually live with: regulars who pay by UPI and stay anonymous, and offers that get lost on WhatsApp.

Delivery marks (pauses, beats, emphasis) will be added when the video is built.

## Script

Your regular paid you 40 times this year.
You still don't know their name.

UPI made paying easy.
It also made you a line in their payment history.
Your WhatsApp offers? Lost under good morning forwards.

Wystak puts your shop in their phone's wallet.
One scan at your counter. No app.
Seconds after they pay, points land on their lock screen.
You see who keeps coming back. And who stopped.

Don't be a line in their payment history.
Be in their wallet.

Wystak. All your passes. One stack.
