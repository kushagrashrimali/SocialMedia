# WYSTAK launch reel v3: "The evolution of loyalty" (merchant, 30s, 9:16)

## The idea

Loyalty has been reinvented once a generation: the stamp, then plastic, then the app. Each time it moved to wherever people paid. Credit cards learned to reward every swipe. India then moved its everyday spending to UPI, and loyalty didn't follow. A shop's regulars pay it every day and earn nothing from it.

WYSTAK is the next step: loyalty that lives where the customer already pays and looks, in the phone's wallet, and updates on every UPI visit.

**The visual spine:** one card object travels through the whole film, changing form in every era without ever cutting away. At the reveal, the three old forms become the **three cards of the WYSTAK logo** and fan out of the W. The logo *is* the history of loyalty, stacked.

## Design direction

- **Ground:** clean light (#F7F8FC, as in the pitch deck) with navy ink. The UPI "nothing" beat goes grey, and the reveal brings the colour back.
- **Colour:** the logo's navy, purple and teal only. Each era object takes one card colour as it heads into the reveal: stamp → navy, plastic → purple, app → teal.
- **Type:** big bold sans for the voiceover words (word-by-word kinetic captions, centred in the 9:16 safe area); a mono face for the era labels and point counts.
- **Motion:** one continuous camera with no hard cuts. Each era object becomes the next one, and the last three become the logo.
- **Sound:** one physical sound per era (stamp thud, plastic snap, app pop, card swipe, UPI chime), then the turn in silence, then Apple-style minimal music under the reveal and demo.

## Claims check

- The rewards the video shows are the shop's own points on its own pass; WYSTAK holds no money and gives no cashback (closed loop).
- The demo shows the real flow: the customer pays by UPI and staff scan the pass at checkout. The video doesn't claim points come straight from the UPI payment with no scan.
- The CRED comparison is made without naming CRED or any bank. "Credit cards learned to reward every swipe" carries it, and the brand rules ban real company names in the video.
- The era decades are approximate history (stamp loyalty began in the 1890s, plastic loyalty cards spread in the 1990s, loyalty apps in the 2010s). They can be dropped if you prefer no dates.
- Fictional merchant: Paper Crane Coffee. No real payment app is shown on the UPI screen.
