## ElevenLabs settings

- **Pronunciation:** the script spells the name **"Why-stack"** so it is said "why-stak". On screen it is always WYSTAK.
- **Voice:** in the Voice Library, search *narration*, *calm*, *premium*, *documentary*. Pick a warm mid-to-low voice with a neutral accent and no "radio ad" energy. Audition the line "Why-stack. All your passes. One stack." first.
- **Model:** Eleven v3 (or Multilingual v2 if v3 sounds too acted).
- **Settings (Multilingual v2):** Stability 55–65 · Similarity 75 · Style 0–10 · Speaker boost on · Speed 0.92–0.95.
- **Voice only:** no music or effects. I add the music and sound design to match the reference video.
- **Export:** WAV if your plan allows, otherwise MP3 at 44.1 kHz, 192 kbps or higher.
- **Pauses:** "..." gives a short pause. If a gap is too short, leave it; I can stretch silences on the timeline. Don't speed the voice up to fit 30s.
- Generate 2–3 takes and send the best one, or all of them and I'll pick.

## Brand checks both scripts pass

- Says "pass" for the wallet object; "card" only means the plastic or paper thing being replaced.
- Says "within seconds", not "under 10 seconds", until the team confirms the measured figure.
- No statistics, prices, expiry, cash-out or cross-merchant points.
- Apple Wallet and Google Wallet get equal weight.
- The merchant shown is the fictional Paper Crane Coffee.
