# WYSTAK loyalty reel: script, "The next place for loyalty" (about 43s, 9:16)

Audience: café, restaurant, salon and shop owners in India. The story follows the brief: payment → recognition → loyalty → data → WhatsApp → too many places → wallet → Wystak. India already went digital on loyalty; it just never gave loyalty one place to live. WhatsApp is part of that progress, not the villain. There are no loyalty cards in the story.

The storytelling follows the reference reel: a big claim about India, then the eras in short lines with one joke each, a quick list cut one object per word, a turn on a black screen, and the brand only at the very end.

## Script (paste into ElevenLabs as is)

```
India didn't stop rewarding customers.
Your customers just stopped noticing.

"Sir, mobile number?"
Businesses learned to remember.
Every visit. Every purchase. Every preference.

And WhatsApp made staying connected effortless.
Salons remembered birthdays better than your cousins.

But loyalty started living everywhere.
An SMS. A chat. An app they deleted.

Another app.
Another login.

What if loyalty didn't need another place?

It already has one. The wallet on their phone.
One scan at your counter. No app.
Seconds after they pay, points land on their lock screen.
Now they notice.

Whys-tak. The next place for loyalty.
```

95 words, about 35s at the launch voice's pace (161 wpm). The brief's silences (the phone locking, black with one ding, the quick recap before the logo, the logo hold) add about 8s in the edit, so the reel lands at about 43s, under 45.

If the take runs long, cut "Salons remembered birthdays better than your cousins." first.
