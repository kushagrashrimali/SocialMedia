# WYSTAK loyalty reel: script v2 (about 51s, 9:16)

Audience: café, restaurant, salon and shop owners in India. India never stopped rewarding customers; loyalty just got scattered across SMS, chats and apps. The wallet already on every phone is the one place it needs. The fictional café in the visuals is Cafe Aroma.

The first recording (the "Sir, mobile number?" version) was replaced by this script after the user's review of the first cut.

## Script (as recorded in ElevenLabs)

```
India didn't stop rewarding customers.
Your customers just stopped noticing.

Every counter started collecting data, not just cash.
Businesses learned to remember.
Every visit. Every purchase. Every preference.

And social media made staying connected effortless.
Cafés remembered birthdays better than your cousins.

But loyalty started living everywhere.
An SMS. A chat. An app they deleted.

Another app.
Another login.

What if loyalty didn't need another place?

It already has one. The wallet on their phone.
One scan at your counter. No app.
Seconds after they pay, points land on their lock screen.
Now they notice.

Why-stack. All your passes. One stack.
```
