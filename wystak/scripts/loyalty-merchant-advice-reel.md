# WYSTAK loyalty reel: merchant-to-merchant version (about 51s, 9:16)

The same story as `loyalty-evolution-reel.md` (India never stopped rewarding customers; loyalty got scattered across SMS, chats and apps; the wallet on every phone is the one place it needs), told by one shop owner to another, ending with a recommendation to get Wystak. No prices, numbers or customer names are claimed; the claims are the allowed ones (no app, scan at the counter, points on the lock screen within seconds of paying, the owner sees who comes back and who stopped).
